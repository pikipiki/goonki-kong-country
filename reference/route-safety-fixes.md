# Canyon cannons and mine encounters — 21 October 2026

The source cannon trig table is signed. Its derived velocity Y is already in the positive-up coordinate frame used by the imported jump velocities. The four generators and four generated level modules formerly inverted it when producing the aim angle; the engine inverted it again when choosing and applying vectors. Both conventions must be corrected together. No launch multipliers or imported source counters were increased.

A separate web adaptation bug ended impulse retention after 0.65 seconds even when source gravity/control gates still ran. `cannonMomentumDuration` retains momentum through the larger source gate plus 1.2 seconds of descent (minimum 1.6 seconds). Real cannon9 at x75 now lands at x≈89.7,y7.5 instead of braking over the gap; real cannon36 and the sequence32→33→34→35 are covered by engine tests without directional input. This does not establish a complete emulator-perfect traversal of all36 cannons, and directional/rotating source behaviors remain partially adapted.

Imported ground sprite coordinates are not guaranteed to be feet. `normalizeLandEnemyFeet` chooses the nearest physical support with full-body clearance at five horizontal samples. No floor is invented. All canyon ground placements are checked.

Real mine Zingers previously had patrol limits extending across the whole455-unit level, allowing encounters to merge. Their patrols now remain within0.6 units of the source anchor and between neighboring source sectors. Source actors and coordinates are preserved. Necky-family obstacles were incorrectly imported with the same invulnerability as Zingers; the main engine/model changes make the birds distinct and stompable.

A targeted mobile adaptation handles the close low/high formations at X269/272 and X372/377/381. Each cluster retains its first low Zinger as a real jump obstacle, lowering it by at most0.4 units without embedding it in the rail. The following high Zingers move into the overhead corridor. These five actors use a fixed vertical height, avoiding arbitrary sinusoidal closure of the jump window; they still patrol laterally in their bounded sectors. Raw source records, same-column stack X297.5 and all other hazards remain unchanged. This is gameplay adaptation, not a claim that those five runtime heights reproduce the SNES placements.

Validation: sixteen route-safety tests cover source-sign cannon trajectories, safe landings, chained cannon capture, full-body enemy placement, patrol separation, the source stacked Zingers and adapted close formations. Each adapted local encounter is crossed with actual8.5-unit/s cart motion and physical collisions at the minimum/middle/maximum initial patrol positions, in both directions. A no-jump attempt against each retained low Zinger takes damage, proving the hazard was not merely neutralized as decoration. The tests establish local encounter reachability and do not replace a complete manual cart playthrough.
